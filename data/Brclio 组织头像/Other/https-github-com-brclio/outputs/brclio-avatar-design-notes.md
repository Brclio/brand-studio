# Brclio organization avatar

## Concept

- **B silhouette** — establishes immediate Brclio recognition at GitHub avatar size.
- **Clio profile** — references the Muse of history, records, and narrative.
- **Laurel** — suggests celebration and making work known.
- **Scroll-shaped C** — connects Clio's initial with writing, archives, tutorials, and knowledge records.
- **Red seal** — a small publishing and archival accent.

The mark uses the Esther Design System palette: blue `#2B7FD8`, yellow `#F4D758`, red `#E84A5F`, warm cream `#FEFCF6`, and ink `#1A1A2E`.

## Generation

Mode: built-in Imagegen.

Final generation brief:

> Create a square GitHub organization avatar for Brclio. Combine a bold capital B silhouette with a simplified Clio side-profile in negative space; let the lower curve unfurl into a scroll-shaped C. Use a strong tiny-size silhouette, restrained crayon hatching, generous margin, and the Esther blue-yellow-red palette. No words, realistic portrait, gradients, 3D, watermark, or decorative clutter.

The selected second pass preserved the original geometry while flattening the palette and removing glow and heavy depth effects.
